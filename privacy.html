Short version

This site sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, runs no analytics and has no accounts. Every file the page loads comes from this site itself, so opening it contacts no advertiser, tracker or content network.

Who is responsible

What happens when you load the page

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare, so GitHub Inc. and Cloudflare, Inc. receive your IP address and record it in server logs in order to deliver the page and to protect it from abuse. Both are United States companies operating from their EU points of presence, and both act under their standard data processing terms. Nothing else is collected, and no record of who visited is kept here.

The pages linked from the front page are separate sites with their own operators. Following a link is a request to whoever runs that site, and this notice does not cover it.

Legal basis and retention

The only processing is the delivery and security logging described above, which relies on legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: a website cannot be served without receiving the request. Retention of those logs is set by GitHub and Cloudflare under their own policies. No separate records are created or kept here.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to personal data concerning you, its correction or erasure, a restriction of processing, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Since no visitor records are kept here, in practice there is nothing to hand over or delete, but write to the address above and it will be answered. You may also complain to a data protection supervisory authority in the country where you live or work.
