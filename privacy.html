Privacy

No cookies, no browser storage, no analytics, no accounts. Every file this page loads comes from this site. The links on the front page go to other people's sites, which have their own terms.

The page is served by GitHub Pages through Cloudflare, so those two see your IP address in their logs in order to deliver it. Nothing else is collected and no record of your visit is kept here.

Jon Shimwell runs this site. Anything you want to ask or object to, including about your own data, goes to mail@jshimwell.com. You can also complain to your national data protection authority.
